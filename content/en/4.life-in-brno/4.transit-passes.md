---
title: Transit passes
description: The student season ticket, contactless payments, the Poseidon app and tickets for the South Moravian Region.
---

To travel around Brno and the surrounding area by public transport, you can use student discounts (the Integrated Transport System of the South Moravian Region, IDS JMK). You can get a discounted season ticket, known as a "šalinkarta", online (it's also cheaper than the paper one). All you need is a contactless bank card that can pay online and a photo in electronic form. You can sort everything out here: [www.brnoid.cz/cs/eoc](https://www.brnoid.cz/cs/eoc).

This way you buy your šalinkarta without queuing: once you've verified your details, you just buy new coupons in the e-shop. Whenever there's a ticket inspector, just hold your contactless card to their device and they'll see your photo and whether your šalinkarta is valid. DPMB will email you in advance before your šalinkarta expires.

For times when you don't have a šalinkarta, it's worth mentioning contactless payments on public transport. All you need is a contactless bank card. When you board a tram/bus/trolleybus, hold your card to the reader by the door. One "beep" lasts an hour, or to the terminus of the current line. If you change, you have to beep again in the new vehicle. If you beep out within 15 minutes of starting your journey, the system changes your ticket to a 15-minute one, and if you collect several 15-minute tickets within an hour, it changes them back to an hourly one. Charges are settled the following night, but the system will never charge you more than a day ticket. If you're an international student, don't worry too much if the payment isn't taken the very next day: international transactions can take up to 14 days. You'll find more information about contactless payments at [pipniajed.cz](https://pipniajed.cz).

For trips around the South Moravian Region outside Brno, you can buy tickets directly from the driver. You can save money by downloading the _Poseidon_ mobile app, which lets you buy all zone tickets valid throughout the South Moravian Region. The good-value tickets are called "universal" tickets, and to get them you need credit in the app (at least the price of the universal ticket). Otherwise the app automatically offers a non-discounted single ticket. In Brno, for example, you can save 5.50 CZK per trip this way. You'll find more information at [idsjmk.cz/a/posedion](https://idsjmk.cz/a/posedion). Universal tickets pay off if you plan to make fewer than 7 trips a week within the given zones of the integrated system. Otherwise a monthly coupon valid for those zones is already worth it. Of course you can also use single and universal tickets alongside your šalinkarta for occasional trips to the suburban zones.

There's also a physical paper version. The standard price is 180 CZK, 90 CZK with a student discount. It has 24 fields to be stamped in the ticket machines and can be used on selected trains and buses within IDS JMK. The specific number of fields to stamp is shown on the ticket. It must be stressed that the 50% student discount does not apply to single or universal tickets for trips within Brno. (For these trips you need to use the fare called Zlevněná B, "Reduced B".) You'll find information about discounts and transport in Brno at [dpmb.cz](https://www.dpmb.cz).

As for trains, on trains marked Os or Sp you can only buy tickets at IDS JMK fares and can't use, for example, ČD (Czech Railways) discounts. On fast trains (rychlík) you can use either IDS JMK tickets (for journeys within the South Moravian Region) or the operator's tickets.
