In the canteens you pay for food with your student card/ISIC. You hold it to the reader, and exactly the price of your meal is deducted from your credit.

You can top up your card in two ways: in cash at the canteen till, or online through the payment gateway. You can now also pay by bank card in the canteens.

You can top up any amount on your student card or ISIC at the canteen, and payments are then deducted from it.

In theory you don't have to use top-ups at all and can pay everything in cash or by bank card, but we don't recommend it: it causes unnecessary queues at the till.

DON'T FORGET to hold your card to the reader even if you know there isn't enough money on it. When you do, the system recognises you as a student and you get your meal at the student price.

The number of meals per person is limited to 3 a day on a monthly average. How you use this limit is up to you.

## Canteens

**Menza Kolejní** – you'll find it in block A02 in the Pod Palackého vrchem dormitory complex. There's always a choice of several dishes and sides, plus one soup. The canteen seats 112 people. Every day it offers a different good-value menu, always published at [www.kam.vutbr.cz](https://www.kam.vutbr.cz). With your main course you get 0.2 l of water for free. Fountain drink cups hold 0.3 l (and 0.5 l).

**Pizzerie Mozzarella** – on the second floor of block A06, next to the "U Žida" grocery. It has a wide range of permanent dishes, such as steaks, pasta and of course pizza. The taste and quality of the pizza match its price, so if you're a pizza lover who cares about value for money, we'd rather recommend Doe Boy. To drink, there are branded fountain drinks, juices and still water.

**Menza Purkyňova** – the canteen of superlatives, in the Purkyňovy dormitory complex. It's the biggest, its queue moves fastest, it has the widest choice of dishes, and according to some it also has the best cooking. There's also a fast-food counter on the ground floor.

**Restaurace Q** – this canteen is in the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering complex. It's ideal if you don't have time to go back to the dorms, or if you're happy with the smaller choice of dishes it offers.

## Snack bars

The snack bar at T10 is a classic snack bar run by KaM, so you can pay with your ISIC there. You can choose from baguettes, various snacks, pastries, tea or coffee. The canteen next to the snack bar is for staff only!

The snack bar and café at T12 and T8 are privately run, so unfortunately you can't pay with your ISIC there yet. The T12 snack bar also has a café where you can enjoy homemade cakes every day, or have a big portion of excellent soup instead of lunch. The soup is different every day, and you'll find the menu for the whole week on the board in the snack bar.
