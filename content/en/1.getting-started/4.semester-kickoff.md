Get in the mood for studying and see what life at FEEC is like.

## PerFEKT Start (11 – 13 September)

The Studenti pro studenty association at the faculty has prepared get-to-know-you events for you. Over the weekend you can look forward to a tour of the faculty and interesting places around the city, along with team-building activities. If you sign up for VUT Předškolovák, you'll meet us even before PerFEKT Start. Four days full of fun, meeting your new classmates and tips for your future studies are waiting for you there.

So unofficially, PerFEKT Start begins at Předškolovák, but if you can't make it there, definitely join us after matriculation. You'll find more information at [perfektstart.cz](https://perfektstart.cz).

## VUT Předškolovák (20 – 23 August and 23 – 26 August)

If you want to meet your new classmates before the semester starts, don't miss this great event. You'll get to know lots of future classmates and other students from all BUT faculties. You'll also meet current students and graduates, who can give you advice on how to get through your studies successfully.

We'll go through the information system with you and tell you what to watch out for and where to start. Don't worry, though, the whole stay definitely won't be just about school.

You can look forward to a packed 4-day programme full of fun, adrenaline attractions, sports, games and parties. All of this in the beautiful surroundings of the Vranov Reservoir, at the most modern campsite in the Czech Republic, so don't hesitate and join us, it'll be worth it! You'll find all the information on the Předškolovák website.

## Hudba z FEKTu 2026

For the past 17 years, on the first Wednesday of the winter semester, a huge stage has appeared out of nowhere in front of the faculty, stalls have gone up, and from 12:00 you could enjoy the Hudba z FEKTu ("Music from FEEC") festival right on the university grounds.

The open-air music festival will take place on 16 September 2026. You can look forward to student bands from FEEC, refreshments, side events and maybe even a surprise or two. Follow the event on Facebook for more information. You'll find more about Hudba z FEKTu in the handbook on page 26 and at [hudbazfektu.cz](https://hudbazfektu.cz).

## Start party

After matriculation, it's important to settle in at the faculty as quickly as possible and pick up the information you need for a fast start to university life. That's why Studenti pro studenty organises a Start party for first-years. You'll meet other FEEC first-years there and, if you're interested, you can also listen to a short presentation about the student web portals, how teaching works and so on. The Start party takes place on the evening of 14 September 2026.
