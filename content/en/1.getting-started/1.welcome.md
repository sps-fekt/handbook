Hi there, university student! You're holding the Freshman handbook, put together for you by Studenti pro studenty (SPS, "Students for students"), a student association at FEEC. It's packed with information that will help you understand how university works, how teaching is organised and, above all, what you need to do and know so you can study at full speed from day one instead of spending half a year finding your feet.

It's definitely worth reading the whole thing, from start to finish. Some of it may not seem important right away, but you never know when it will come in handy.

Don't forget to read it again before you start. The summer is long, it's easy to forget things, and you'll need this information most at the very beginning of your studies.

However hard we try, there will always be situations this handbook doesn't cover. When that happens, you have three options: ask on our faculty Discord, write to us at SPS, or visit the Study Office, where the study officers will help you.

So if you have any questions, or you find that something important is missing from the handbook, don't hesitate to email us at [info@sps-fekt.cz](mailto:info@sps-fekt.cz) or message us on Instagram [@spsfekt](https://www.instagram.com/spsfekt).

All that's left is to wish you a great summer and lots of personal and academic success.

**Studenti pro studenty**

## The most important things

Join our faculty Discord at [sps-fekt.cz/discord](https://sps-fekt.cz/discord). That way we can answer any further questions, and you can get in touch with new people from all over the faculty.

Your timetable will be assigned to you, and it can't be changed in the first semester.

When communicating with FEEC staff, always use your university email address, which has the format [id@vut.cz](mailto:id@vut.cz).

Staff are not obliged to reply to emails from other addresses.
