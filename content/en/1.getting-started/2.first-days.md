## Official notices

The faculty publishes all important documents and information on its website. It's important to read them: you'll find everything there, such as when to come to matriculation and other mandatory events. The hardest part is usually finding them. Go to [www.fekt.vut.cz](https://www.fekt.vut.cz), open the menu For students (Pro studenty), and there you'll find Study regulations and internal standards (Studijní předpisy a vnitřní normy).

[www.fekt.vut.cz/pro_studenty/studijni_predpisy](https://www.fekt.vut.cz/pro_studenty/studijni_predpisy)

## Matriculation

Matriculation is the ceremony that formally admits you to the academic community. It will take place on 11 September in Professor Brauner's auditorium at Technická 12 (the big dome, also known as the Death Star). Make sure you come in formal clothing, i.e. a suit, tie or dress. Exact information, such as the time and how students are split into groups, will be published in advance in a notice on the faculty website.

::warning
**NOTE:** Matriculation is for incoming students only, not for parents. Even if they come, they will unfortunately have to stay outside the auditorium because of limited capacity.
::

## Mandatory safety training and qualification

To take part in laboratory classes during your studies, you'll need to complete the mandatory safety training and qualification. It will take place according to the instructions published on the website of the Department of Electrical Power Engineering (UTEE).

## Student card / ISIC

Your student card is an important part of life at FEEC. You'll use it to open the doors to classrooms, pay for food in the canteen, and prove your identity at exams and tests. You can choose one of two cards: the standard student card, which is all you need around the faculty, or an ISIC, which also gets you all kinds of student discounts, such as state transport discounts, Student Agency, Alza, accommodation discounts across Europe, cheaper museum entry, travel, insurance, sport and other benefits, see [isic.cz](https://www.isic.cz). The student card is free; the ISIC costs an extra 250 CZK and is valid for one year.

It's essential to order your card (student card or ISIC) as soon as possible. You can place the order in the information system under the Portal tab.

First you'll have to upload your photo to the system. It must be in colour, passport format 4.5 × 3.5 cm, on a light background.

As a first-year student who doesn't yet have a standard student card, you can get an ISIC at a reduced price. You can order the free student card from the beginning of August.

You'll get an email at your university address once the card is ready for collection. You can pick it up from the beginning of September, following the instructions in the information system.

## MUNI card – ONLY for the BPC-BTB programme

At your first class at the Faculty of Medicine of Masaryk University, they'll take your photo and have a student card made for you. They'll let you know by email when it's ready. This card costs 120 CZK. Together with the card you'll receive your UČO (universal person number) and a primary and secondary password. Keep all of them safe! Your UČO and primary password are used to log in to the MU information system ([is.muni.cz](https://is.muni.cz)); the secondary password is for the MU Union Catalogue ([aleph.muni.cz](https://aleph.muni.cz)) and for logging in to computers in MU study rooms. It can also be used to connect to the eduroam network (Wi-Fi); the login name is your MUNI email address (UČO@mail.muni.cz).

You'll mainly use this card to enter rooms and in libraries.

## Who to turn to with problems

All sorts of problems can come up during your studies, whether they're related to your studies, questions about studying, or any kind of certificate. All of this is handled by the study officer at the Study Office.

Your year is looked after by the study officer assigned to your study programme. For more complex problems, the faculty has a Vice-Dean for Study Affairs, doc. Ing. Vlasta Sedláková, Ph.D. She also deals with more serious matters, such as an individual study plan. However, always start at the Study Office, which will refer you to the Vice-Dean if needed.

Under no circumstances should you go to the Dean with ordinary study matters; that's what the Study Office is for.

If you run into other problems during your studies, don't be afraid to use the other forms of social support BUT offers (e.g. psychological help or help with resolving conflicts at BUT), see <https://www.vut.cz/vut/podpora-zamestnancu/socialni-bezpeci>. It provides help and prevention for students and staff alike.
