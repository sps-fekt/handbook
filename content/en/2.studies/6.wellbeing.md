In your studies and your personal life there may be situations you don't know how to handle on your own. BUT cares about its students and offers you FREE psychological counselling. The Centre of Lifelong Learning employs a number of experienced psychologists who help both with ordinary difficult situations and with crises.

Don't worry that you won't be able to put your feelings into words: the psychologists are experienced in dealing with such problems and know how to ask the right questions to find out what help you need. There's nothing to be ashamed of; mental health and wellbeing need to be looked after just as carefully as physical health. We believe you agree. :)

::note
**Don't hesitate to reach out to them if you're asking yourself any of these questions:**

- **I'm stressed and don't know what to do about it.**
- **I'm going through a hard time and would like to talk to someone about it.**
- **I'm having trouble with my studies and want to face it.**
- **My relationships with friends, partners, parents or people around me aren't how I'd like them to be.**
- **I don't really understand myself, I don't know what I want.**
- **I want to work on myself, but I don't know how.**
::
