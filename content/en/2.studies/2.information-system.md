You'll hear some people call it Apollo, others simply the IS. It's the BUT information system, which accompanies every student throughout their studies, and some even beyond, into their nightmares.

This guide is here to help you get to know it faster. And if you read it again after a few months, it may still reveal some tips and tricks you hadn't noticed before.

## What is the IS actually for?

In it you'll find your VUT PIN, order your ISIC, arrange accommodation in the dormitories, enrol in courses, schedule your lectures, exercises, labs and exams, communicate with teachers, and find study materials, your electronic index with points from exams and tests, and a clear graphical timetable. But you'll also find lots of tabs you won't need at all in your first year, which can make the IS confusing.

The first thing you'll come across is logging in. You should have received all the information you need together with your admission decision or by email. If not, don't hesitate to contact the Study Office; they'll be happy to help. Specifically, you'll be given an initial VUT password, which we recommend you don't lose. Of course you can change the password, but you're 100% guaranteed to need it again! The initial password is also useful for logging in to the university systems.

Once you log in, you'll see that the system is divided into tabs, Portal, Studis, Teacher, eLearning and Apollo, plus a menu on the left. You'll mostly use the Studis and eLearning tabs regularly. At the start, the Portal tab will also be useful for settings and other essentials.

**The Portal tab** always shows your received VUT messages first, which is nice for an overview, but you'll want to set up forwarding to your personal email that you check regularly anyway. You can also set up sending messages from Gmail via SMTP, so you don't have to go into the IS to email your teachers.

What's really important in the Portal tab, though, is your VUT PIN, which you'll use to log in to the Wi-Fi. It's worth learning it by heart. You'll find it next to your name.

This is also where you'll order one of the mandatory cards (ISIC or student card) in the Cards (Průkazy) menu. In the My business card (Moje vizitka) menu you can set up forwarding of messages to your email by ticking a single checkbox. It's better, though, to set up forwarding in the email interface as well.

The last thing you'll care about in this tab is Accommodation and catering (Ubytování a stravování). It takes you to a separate IS for KaM (the BUT Accommodation and Catering Service), where you can enter your bank account number, find and book a room, sign the contract digitally and so on.

![The Portal tab in the BUT information system with highlighted areas: Important tabs, Ordering a student card, Booking accommodation, Wi-Fi password](/images/is-portal.webp)

**Studis** will be a much more interesting tab for you. This is where you enrol in courses and later register for exam dates, and where you'll find your timetable and index. When you open the Student tab, you land on the Introductory information page. It includes recently launched modules, some of which may be extra and may not concern you yet.

Below the introductory information you'll find a link to the Personal information and Studies module, where you can switch between programmes if you study more than one.

You'll definitely appreciate the menu item called Individual timetable (Individuální rozvrh), which tells you where you need to be, when, and which course is coming up. Clicking on teachers takes you to their contact details, and you can easily click through to the course page as well.

![Individual timetable in the BUT information system](/images/is-rozvrh.webp){width="300"}

Another important module in the IS is the Electronic index. Teachers will (or won't) enter points for tests there, award credits and grade the semester. Don't panic if not all your credits have a green tick; lots of teachers leave it until the last minute. Teachers will tell you in the first classes what you need to do to get the credit for their course. You can keep track in the index of whether you're meeting all of it. You'll always find out your exam results, including the grade and points for the semester. They appear in the IS as soon as the teacher has marked everything.

![Electronic index in the BUT information system](/images/is-index.webp)

Also worth mentioning are the course news (aktuality), where you can find some study materials or test dates, but above all, when you're standing with your classmates in front of a locked lab door wondering where the teacher is, this is where you'll notice the announcement that the class has been cancelled. (Teachers shouldn't use it any more and should put everything in eLearning instead, but it may be worth checking it now and then just in case.)

Most teachers put their materials (PDFs to study from, e-textbooks, presentations) on the course page (click the course in the electronic index or individual timetable) and in eLearning. If you can't find anything in either place, try looking in the course news mentioned above.

## When exams are coming up

The Exam date registration (Registrace termínů) module is for registering for credit tests and exams. It will bring you plenty of stress, so listen carefully in the first class of every course, where, according to the rules, the credit test date must be announced. As for exams, they're always correct in the IS.

The number of people who can register for an exam date is limited, so as soon as registration opens for a course, you need to be quick and check everything regularly.

All exam dates must be announced **THREE WEEKS BEFORE THE START OF THE EXAM PERIOD**, otherwise it's a breach of the Study and Examination Regulations. If a teacher misses this, you can politely ask them to announce the dates, or contact SPS ([sps-fekt.cz](https://sps-fekt.cz)). The filters in exam date registration will help you check whether you really are registered for dates for all your exams.

![Exam date registration in the BUT information system](/images/is-registrace.webp)

If you haven't managed to prepare for an exam, you can deregister from the date up to a certain deadline. This option doesn't exist, however, for credit tests or deadlines for semester projects, labs and so on.

Early on you'll also use the Scholarships and fees (Stipendia a poplatky) module. Register your bank account there, print out the confirmation and take it to the Study Office. If you're not from Brno, also apply for the accommodation scholarship, which is only paid at the end of the semester, but it's better to get everything sorted as soon as possible. We recommend going through this whole module and sorting everything out together with your accommodation and ISIC, so it's all done in one go.

Some people may also find the links in the Counselling and courses (Poradenství a kurzy) module useful. You'll find lots of interesting information there, including advice on how to get a social scholarship if you find yourself in a difficult situation.

**eLearning** is a tab that will be simple in your first semester, because you'll only have a few courses open. They're used to share files from teachers; you'll find all the information about the course and how it's organised, some study materials, and for some courses the exam questions will appear here towards the end of the semester. Online tests may also take place here, but your teachers will tell you about that.

## Check of completed courses

A module in Studis that helps you keep track of what you've completed and what you still need to complete.

You'll find the number of credits you've earned, the limits you need to meet, and also how compulsory elective courses are split into groups, each with its own requirements.

During the semester, and especially before it starts, stop here for a moment so that you don't end up missing a credit or a compulsory elective course at the end of your studies.

**We at SPS wish you lots of patience and strong nerves with the BUT information system. At first it will be a big maze for you, but you'll find your way around quickly.**
