To find your way around the academic world, it helps to know the following terms. Your secondary school was probably headed by a headteacher with deputies. FEEC and all the other BUT faculties have a Dean and Vice-Deans who represent the Dean in particular areas. At university level, above them, there's the Rector and the Vice-Rectors.

## University governing bodies

### Rector

Heads the university. Acts and decides on the university's affairs. The Rector's term of office is four years, and the same person may hold this office at the same public university for at most two consecutive terms.

### Vice-Rectors

The Rector chooses Vice-Rectors who represent them in particular areas. At BUT, for example, there are Vice-Rectors for international and external relations, for study, for science and research, and for strategy.

### BUT Academic Senate

The Academic Senate (AS) of a public university is the highest representative self-governing body of the university and has a major influence on the whole university and all its faculties. Its members are elected from the academic community for 3-year terms; it has a chamber of academic staff and a student chamber.

Above all, the Academic Senate proposes the appointment and dismissal of the Rector to the President of the Republic, or of the Dean to the Rector; decides, on the Rector's proposal, on establishing, merging or dissolving parts of the university (a faculty, for example, is such a part); and approves the budget and internal regulations (including study regulations). The student chamber also maintains contact with the university leadership and passes on suggestions from students (and student associations).

You'll find more information at [vutbr.cz/vut/struktura/as](https://www.vutbr.cz/vut/struktura/as).

## Faculty governing bodies

### Dean

The faculty is headed by the Dean, who acts and decides on the faculty's affairs. The Dean is appointed and dismissed by the Rector on the proposal of the faculty's Academic Senate. The Dean's term of office is four years.

### Vice-Dean

The Vice-Deans represent the Dean to the extent the Dean determines. Each Vice-Dean takes care of a particular area, such as study, science and research, or external relations. Vice-Deans are appointed and dismissed by the Dean.

### Faculty Secretary

Manages the faculty's finances, operations and internal administration.

### Dean's Board

This body consists of members of the faculty leadership, heads of departments, the chair of the faculty Academic Senate, a student representative and possibly other people. It meets regularly and discusses matters related to the running and development of the faculty and, by extension, the university. Most strategic plans are born and discussed here.

### Disciplinary Committee

It's appointed by the Dean and deals with any offences by students against the university's internal regulations. If, for example, you cheat in an exam or behave inappropriately towards another member of the academic community, the Disciplinary Committee may propose to the Dean, for instance, conditional expulsion. At FEEC the committee has six members, half of whom are students.

### Faculty Academic Senate

The faculty Academic Senate is the faculty's self-governing representative academic body. At FEEC it consists of 12 representatives of the individual departments, 6 representatives of bachelor's and master's students and 1 representative of doctoral students. Meetings of the faculty Academic Senate are open to the public. The faculty Academic Senate approves proposals for the faculty's internal regulations and the allocation of the faculty's funds and checks how they're used, approves the admission requirements for the study programmes run by the faculty, votes on the proposal to appoint the Dean or proposes their dismissal, and approves the faculty's long-term plan.

The whole faculty hierarchy is shown quite nicely here: [fekt.vut.cz/o_fakulte/organizacni_struktura](https://www.fekt.vut.cz/o_fakulte/organizacni_struktura)

## How to address academic staff correctly

At FEEC you'll meet people whose list of titles is three times longer than their name. You may find yourself wanting to contact someone without knowing how to address them correctly. Besides titles, there are also positions, just to make it less simple.

Positions: e.g. Dean, Vice-Dean, chair of the Academic Senate, Faculty Secretary, head of department, director of something, etc.

If you're writing to someone in connection with their position (e.g. to a head of department because a teacher referred you to them, or to the Dean because the Study Office referred you), address them by that position, e.g. "Dear Dean", "Dear Vice-Dean" (in Czech: Vážený pane děkane, vážená paní proděkanko).

If you're writing to someone about teaching or anything else, which will probably happen more often, address them by their highest title, which, from highest down, is:

- Professor – prof.
- Associate professor (docent) – doc.
- Doctor – Ph.D.
- Engineer/master – Ing./Mgr.

The exceptions are the Candidate of Sciences (CSc.) and the bachelor's degree (Bc.). The CSc. is no longer awarded, but it's on the same level as a Ph.D. Purely for practical reasons, because "Dear Candidate of Sciences" is a long form of address, this title is ignored when it's someone's highest, and you address them as Ing.
