At BUT, and so at FEEC, there are several ways to get some money. The easiest is scholarships: accommodation, merit, social and extraordinary social scholarships.

You'll find all of them at: [vutbr.cz/studenti/stipendia](https://www.vutbr.cz/studenti/stipendia)

**What do you need?**

- Be a BUT student.
- Have an account with a bank in the Czech Republic.
- Have that account registered at BUT.
- Apply for the scholarship in time.

**General conditions:**

You register your scholarship account in the information system under Scholarships -> scholarship account settings (Stipendia -> nastavení stipendijního účtu). Enter your account number there. If you don't live in the dormitories, you'll have to print out the confirmation and hand it in at the Study Office. Then you can apply for the scholarship you're entitled to. All scholarships must be paid to the same account.

Students studying at several universities at the same time lose their entitlement to the BUT accommodation and social scholarships if they already receive them at another university.

International students must open an account with a Czech bank if they want to pay dormitory fees and claim scholarships.

## Accommodation scholarship

The **accommodation scholarship** can only be awarded to students who:

- study full-time within the standard length of study,
- are studying their 1st study programme,
- have their permanent residence outside the Brno-City and Brno-Country districts.

The whole application is submitted online, and if your account is registered, you don't need to go anywhere. The application for the accommodation scholarship is automatically extended to the next semester.

The amount is decided by the Rector based on how much money comes from the Ministry. It's usually around 540 CZK a month.

The accommodation scholarship is paid in one go for a 4-month cycle, but the amount may change every semester.

## Merit scholarship

An investment in the future. As a first-year student you can't apply for this scholarship.

It's calculated from your average in the previous year, so do your best to get it next year! :-)

Required credits: 45 credits per year.\
This scholarship is usually paid every month.

## Social scholarship

You can apply for this scholarship if you're under 26, you're entitled to child benefit, and at the same time the relevant family income doesn't exceed 1.5 times the family's subsistence minimum.

The child benefit must be awarded by the Czech social welfare authority, not abroad!

If you meet these conditions, you also need a confirmation from the Labour Office (not a bank statement or the overview of benefits already paid shown in the table) and a completed scholarship application (you'll find it on the social scholarship pages). All of this then has to be brought to the Study Office.

You need to request the confirmation from the Labour Office. You must keep an eye on changes in your entitlement to the scholarship, in a document called "Statement for the purposes of awarding a scholarship" (Sdělení pro účely přiznání stipendia), and report them to the Study Office, because as soon as your benefits are reduced, your entitlement to the scholarship ends and any money paid in excess has to be returned.

This scholarship is also paid in 4-month cycles.

## Extraordinary social scholarship

BUT looks after its students who find themselves in a difficult life situation. A one-off social scholarship is available for them. For the purposes of this scholarship, a difficult life situation means a significant weakening or loss of the ability of the student or their family to cover the costs of their studies or to meet study obligations because of sudden changes in the life of the student or their family (a death in the family, loss of housing, etc.). You can read more about this scholarship in Rector's Decision No. 20/2017.

The accommodation scholarship, social scholarship and the scholarship for a difficult life situation are awarded and paid by the Rector's Office ([stipendium@vut.cz](mailto:stipendium@vut.cz)); the merit scholarship is awarded and paid by the faculty ([rockova@vut.cz](mailto:rockova@vut.cz)).
