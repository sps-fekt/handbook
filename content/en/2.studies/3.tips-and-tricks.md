## Discord server

A few years ago we set up a Discord server for all FEEC students, where you'll find everything you need for your studies, but also for social life at the faculty.

Stuck on maths or electrical engineering, or don't know where to go for a beer? Don't despair, you'll find out all of that in one of the many channels on the server, where you can help each other with classmates, remind each other of exam dates, or chat with people from different years, programmes or other universities.

How do I get on the Discord? We've dedicated a [whole page](/en/faculty/discord) of the handbook to Discord, as well as the page **[sps-fekt.cz/discord](https://sps-fekt.cz/discord)**.

## Calculator and printer

![Casio calculator](/images/kalkulacka.webp){width="140"}

Your calculator will become a very close friend during your studies. So choose it very carefully, so that you don't have to replace it halfway through. One of the functions your calculator must have is calculating with complex numbers. Matrix calculations are also handy. CAREFUL: calculators that can plot functions are great, but teachers don't like them in exams and usually ban them, so sometimes less is more. The calculator that probably meets all the requirements best is the Casio FX 991 ES PLUS, or its more modern version FX 991 EX, or the FX 991 CE X.

During your studies you'll also have to print quite a lot of lab reports and other materials. You can easily print, for example, in the library or in the dormitories, where you can use a printing machine or ask a student. Some students in the dormitories offer printing services at very good prices almost non-stop. Just ask (or search) in the dormitory channels on Discord or the dormitory Facebook groups.

But if you have an unused printer lying around at home and you're wondering whether you'll use it, definitely bring it with you. Studying is much more comfortable that way.

## Logging in to the system

To set up access to the BUT information system for the first time, log in at [login.vutbr.cz](https://login.vutbr.cz) with your birth number (foreign students, including from the CZ, use their birth code) and your initial password. The initial password is given in your electronic application. This gets you your personal number (VUT ID), a personal six-digit number used as your login name. You'll also be able to change the initial password to whatever you want. With this password you can then log in to the IS, your email or the computer labs.

As if that weren't enough, logging in to the university Wi-Fi uses your VUT PIN. It's generated by the system; you'll find it after logging in to the information system, in the Portal, by clicking your name and then "VUT PIN".

See [www.vut.cz/intra/vut-pin](https://www.vut.cz/intra/vut-pin).

## Connecting to the internet

There are two Wi-Fi networks at the faculty: VUT Brno and eduroam. VUT Brno is the older network, but its advantage is easier login: just connect, open your browser and it will redirect you to the login page. You log in with your VUT ID and VUT PIN. BUT websites work on this network even without logging in. The downside is that you have to log in again every time you connect.

Eduroam is a bit more complicated to set up, but after the first login it connects automatically. Its huge advantage is that the network is available on academic premises almost all over the world. Abroad, it can sometimes save your neck. You'll find guides on how to connect to eduroam on Windows, Android or Apple in the [BUT user documentation](https://doc.vut.cz/en/WifiSiteTelefony/Eduroam).

Outside the BUT network, you connect to the network drives that are only available from it through the VPN. The [official guide](https://doc.vut.cz/en/WifiSiteTelefony/VPN) covers Windows 11, Windows 10, macOS and OpenVPN, but not Linux. On Linux, you can use the unofficial guide or script at [VUT-VPN-Linux](https://github.com/vofy/VUT-VPN-Linux).

## Free Microsoft products

The university has prepared a gift for all students: legal Microsoft developer software for free. This includes Windows itself, but not Office. That comes in a separate package, Office 365. You'll find more information on the Intraportal. You log in to Office 365 at [office.com](https://www.office.com) with your IS password and \<vutID>@vutbr.cz.

## Preparing for labs

From the second week on, some teachers will ask you for a preparation for the lab task they assign you in the first week. So what is this preparation? A lab task assignment usually looks like this: the task, a theoretical introduction, and the measurement procedure. The preparation is a summary of the theoretical introduction in your own words. Usually one to two pages are expected, but your teachers will tell you all that. A few sentences definitely won't do as a preparation, though!

It's very practical to write the preparation as part of your future lab report. So you write the title of the task, the assignment and the theoretical introduction (the preparation), and take that to the lab. After the measurements you just add the results and you're done. No need to write the whole report from scratch.

## Textbooks

FEEC makes sure that as many textbooks as possible are available electronically. You'll find all the textbooks for your courses in eLearning or on the course page. Definitely don't go hunting for printed textbooks in advance. Firstly, they hardly exist any more, and secondly, you won't need them in advance. You'll find out during the semester which textbooks are worth having on paper. Having them on paper usually means printing them out from the PDF.
