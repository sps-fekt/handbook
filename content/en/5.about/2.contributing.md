## Editing in the browser

1. Open [/_studio](/_studio) and sign in with your `@sps-fekt.cz` Google account. A site admin has to grant you access.
2. Edit the page. You see a preview right away.
3. Click **Publish**. The change is committed to the repository and shows up on the site within about 5 minutes.

## Editing with Git

The repository is [sps-fekt/handbook](https://github.com/sps-fekt/handbook). Pages are Markdown files in the `content/` folder. See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for the workflow.

## Translation

- Czech is the source. Every change starts in `content/cs/`.
- The English version has **the same file name** in `content/en/`.
- The [/en/translations](/en/translations) dashboard shows which pages need translating or updating.

::tip
Only fixing a typo, so the translation doesn't change? Put `lunaria-ignore` in the commit message, and the page won't be marked as outdated.
::
