What to do when you want to stretch your legs after long lectures and take your mind off things? That's what CESA (the Centre of Sports Activities) is for. It offers around 76 sports, from the best known such as football, ice hockey and basketball to martial arts, yoga, frisbee, zumba and more. BUT has several sports grounds in Brno, Pod Palackého vrchem. Two sports a week are completely free; others cost a fee. Some sports are always paid, though (you pay for renting an external sports facility, from around 400 CZK per semester).

To sign up for sports, you'll first need to log in with your VUT ID and VUT password. If you don't know them, follow the instructions on this page: [https://www.vutbr.cz/cvis/navody/vut-login](https://www.vutbr.cz/cvis/navody/vut-login).

At the start of every semester, electronic registration for classes of the optional physical education course takes place in Studis. You'll find more information under Sport. Students can cancel the sports they've registered for during the first two weeks of teaching. Registered sports show up in your timetable in the information system. But signing up for the sport you really want is sometimes harder than it seems. It's more like a clicking war, and whoever's page loads first wins. Within the first ten minutes after registration opens, almost all sports are taken. It's also no longer possible to sign up for classes directly at the sports grounds or with the teachers!

The prices of individual sports are shown when you sign up. You'll find out exactly when registration starts at [www.cesa.vutbr.cz](https://www.cesa.vutbr.cz). That website also has information about everything to do with sports at BUT.
