## Faculty campuses

![Aerial view of the faculty campuses T08, T10 and T12](/images/arealy-fakulty.webp)

It's good to know where your classroom is. At Technická 8 and Technická 10 it's easy: go to the right floor, given by the first digit of the room number, and then finding the room is no problem. The only exception is the auditorium at T10, which is listed in the system as Professor Kalendovský's auditorium (Aula profesora Kalendovského), but you'll easily find it on the ground floor right opposite the reception.

Technická 12 is a different story. It's divided into separate buildings A–H. The most universal advice would be: go up the stairs behind the entrance door and then follow the signs on the ceiling. You'll find the auditorium and buildings A, B, F and G on the right, and the rest on the left. First you need to find the right building, and only then the floor. You can't get, for example, from the 6th floor of building A to the 6th floor of building C.

## FEEC library

![FEEC library](/images/knihovna-fekt.webp)

The faculty library is on the ground floor of the T12 building. After you come in through the main entrance, go through the glass doors on the left, and the library door is right behind them on the right.

The library isn't just for borrowing books, though. It has internet, PCs, desks with power sockets and good Wi-Fi coverage, just like everywhere else at the faculty. Visitors should keep quiet in the library; otherwise it doesn't matter whether you're sitting at a desk working on a lab report, reading the latest Forbes or just hanging out with classmates.

You can also print in the library.

## Central Library

![BUT Central Library](/images/ustredni-knihovna.webp)

The BUT Central Library is at the Faculty of Business and Management, Kolejní 4. To find the entrance, walk past the reception and go down the stairs, where you'll see a sign with an arrow. Unlike the faculty library, it also offers team study rooms (e.g. for group study or working on projects). You can book study rooms through the information system at [https://www.vut.cz/intra/rezervace-mistnosti](https://www.vut.cz/intra/rezervace-mistnosti). Besides the usual books and periodicals, the Central Library also manages electronic resources, such as databases of scientific articles and Czech technical standards (ČSN). Both are free for students. You'll find more information on the library website [https://www.vut.cz/uk](https://www.vut.cz/uk). You can also buy BUT hoodies here.

## Student lounge at T12

![Student lounge at T12](/images/studentarium-t12.webp)

A place to have lunch, make some tea, wait for a lecture or do your homework: that's the student lounge (Studentárium) at T12. Just take the stairs next to the reception, turn right, and the first door on the left takes you into the Studentárium.

There's a microwave, a kettle and basic dishes. Don't forget to wash up after yourself and don't take anything away, so that others can use it too. :)

## Student lounge at T8

On the 2nd floor of Technická 8, on the left, you'll find lounges that are open all day (6:00–22:00) to all students who need some peace and quiet to study. The lounges are T2.45 and T2.55. In the rooms you'll find a whiteboard, markers and even a microwave. We keep trying to improve the rooms and add equipment, so you can look forward to more things that make your time at the faculty nicer.

## Study Office

![Faculty building](/images/studijni-oddeleni.webp)

Student cards, confirmations of study, confirmations for student travel discounts, collecting student cards and lots of other matters are handled at the Study Office. They simply have the university's stamp, and whenever you need it for anything, drop by during their office hours, which you'll find at [fekt.vut.cz/o_fakulte/organizacni_struktura/dek_so](https://www.fekt.vut.cz/o_fakulte/organizacni_struktura/dek_so). The information will certainly also be in the news on the faculty website. The Study Office also handles matters such as issuing confirmations of study, a more flexible study plan, ending your studies and so on. Don't be afraid to drop by if you're dealing with something our handbook doesn't answer.

You'll find them on the second floor of Technická 10, behind a glass-walled area with several tables and chairs.

## Faculty of Medicine, Masaryk University (for BPC-BTB)

The MU campus, where part of the BPC-BTB programme is taught, is in Bohunice. In your individual timetable it's marked as "Kamenice". From FEEC you can get there by bus 72, changing at the Královo Pole, nádraží stop to the direct E56 bus, or by tram 12 to Konečného náměstí, where you change to trolleybus 25.

![Masaryk University building](/images/lf-mu.webp)

At MU you'll need a white lab coat for the labs, so get one before the semester starts.

## JAMU (for BPC-AUD)

Most courses are taught right at FEEC, but for some you'll have to commute to the Faculty of Music of JAMU (the Janáček Academy of Performing Arts). It's on Komenského náměstí in front of the Red Church. You'll recognise it by the fountain in front of the entrance. As it's close to the Česká stop, getting there is easy: tram 12 from FEEC. You don't need any card to enter and move around JAMU.

Your study tools also differ from other programmes. For music theory, any keyboard will definitely come in handy. A two-octave MIDI keyboard that you connect to your PC is enough. You'll also use good headphones and a music notation program on your computer (Sibelius, MuseScore).

## Faculty of Law, Masaryk University (for BPC-IBE)

Most courses are taught at FEEC, and some at the MU Faculty of Law, which is very easy to reach by tram 12. You can get off at Klusáčkova or Nerudova; the Faculty of Law is not far from the BUT Faculty of Civil Engineering, next to Björnsonův sad park.

A few courses are taught together with the Faculty of Business and Management, which is a brisk 6-minute walk from FEEC.
