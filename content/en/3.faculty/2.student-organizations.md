There are many different associations at Brno University of Technology that fit into this category. Some work across the whole university, others only locally (at individual faculties). Below we introduce only those that can have a direct impact on every FEEC student.

## Studenti pro studenty (SPS)

Hi, that's us! A bunch of people who don't want to just sit at their computers in the dorms and enjoy doing meaningful things that benefit students. We try to make it easier for first-years to start at our faculty, for example with this handbook or our Discord server. We try to fill everyone's free time meaningfully with cultural, sports and fun events, and we also try to make sure information flows well and efficiently at the faculty. We have a website, [sps-fekt.cz](https://sps-fekt.cz), and run an Instagram page, [instagram.com/spsfekt](https://www.instagram.com/spsfekt).

You can get to know us during PerFEKT Start or Předškolovák, but we officially open the new academic year together on the first Wednesday of the winter semester, when our biggest festival, Hudba z FEKTu, takes place between the T10 and T12 buildings.

If you'd like to enjoy university and help others, we're looking for you! Join us; there's no commitment. You can approach us at any event we organise, on our website [sps-fekt.cz](https://sps-fekt.cz), on Instagram or at [info@sps-fekt.cz](mailto:info@sps-fekt.cz).

## Student chamber of the FEEC Academic Senate

![Logo of the student chamber of the Academic Senate](/images/logo-skas.webp){width="240"}

The Academic Senate always consists of a chamber of academic staff and a student part. And the student chamber of the Academic Senate (SKAS) is one of the first places you can turn to without any problem when you need help.

The student representatives in the Academic Senate are there above all to defend the interests of all the faculty's students in its meetings, especially when approving internal regulations and some notices that affect students directly (e.g. the study, examination, scholarship and disciplinary regulations, admission requirements, etc.). Besides that, they can help students individually with all kinds of study-related problems. Either they'll "just" advise you on what to do in a particular situation, who to turn to and so on, or in serious cases they can take steps themselves to improve your situation.

The advantage is that student senators have certain powers by law that let them actually solve even complex problems. They usually know what's going on at the faculty and often communicate with its leadership, but above all they're students like you. That means they'll be able to put themselves in your shoes. You definitely don't need to be afraid of them. The title "senator" may sound grand, but in this case it's unnecessarily lofty. Don't be afraid to get in touch. :)

See: [fekt.vutbr.cz/o_fakulte/akademicky_senat](https://www.fekt.vutbr.cz/o_fakulte/akademicky_senat)

There's also the student chamber of the BUT Academic Senate, with one student representative from each faculty. It defends students' interests at the highest level. It's also worth adding that members of the Academic Senate are elected in regular elections once every 3 years, and every member of the academic community, including you, has the right both to vote and to stand for election!

SPS, the FEEC SKAS and the university SKAS work closely together. If you need to turn to one of them, you don't need to worry much about which one you choose. We'll sort it out between us.

## BEST Brno

![BEST Brno logo](/images/logo-best.webp){width="200"}

Want to make the most of your studies? Find friends all over BUT? Boost your skill set? That's what BEST Brno is for!

During the year we organise educational and fun events for students and our members. With us you can have fun, go to workshops (even at companies) and grow at the same time. Get a head start on your career and something for your CV = _ULTIMATE SELF-DEVELOPMENT!_

Thought about Erasmus but have doubts? Try travelling first to the shorter BEST courses around Europe, which last 7–11 days. A mix of learning, travelling and fun awaits you that makes suspiciously good sense. You can sign up as early as your first year. And don't worry, your wallet won't have a meltdown.

More information at: [_best-brno.cz_](https://best-brno.cz)

## YSpace

![YSpace logo](/images/logo-yspace.webp){width="220"}

YSpace is a student team at FEEC BUT that brings together students interested in space technology. It lets them take part in projects through which they can broaden their knowledge, gain valuable experience and be involved in things that don't just end in class but can end up in orbit. If you're drawn to hands-on engineering and want to be part of projects with real results, you'll feel at home at YSpace.

In the team you can get involved in hardware development, programming, system design, integration and testing of technologies that are part of a real engineering project. It's especially a place for students interested in electronics, embedded systems and software. Nobody expects you to know everything from the start. All you need is the will to learn, try new things and not be afraid to get involved.

For first-years, YSpace can be a great opportunity to become part of a community of people who want to do something extra right from the beginning. Besides new skills, you'll gain teamwork experience, contacts and the chance to be part of projects that aim to push student engineering further. If you enjoy technology, want to work on yourself and are drawn to seeing the results of your work in the real world, YSpace may be exactly the place to start.

More at: [_linktr.ee/yspace_brno_](https://linktr.ee/yspace_brno)

## Drone Research Center

![Drone Research Center logo](/images/logo-drone-research-center.webp){width="200"}

We're a new creative student team at BUT: an innovative student team focused on unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), combining talent from all over BUT and based at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Communication. Since 2020 we've had experience with research projects developing drone swarms for the Ministry of Defence / Army of the Czech Republic. Our goal is to develop autonomous drones and software to control them. We work with companies on their projects and take part in international competitions.

More at: [utee.fekt.vut.cz/en/drone-research-center](https://utee.fekt.vut.cz/en/drone-research-center)

## Brno Mars Rover

We're a team of active and ambitious students from Brno University of Technology. Our long-standing desire to be part of something that eventually goes beyond our research group or even our whole department led us to discover the ultimate robotics challenge. Combined with our passion for everything space-related, it was the obvious choice.

At the end of 2023 we started preparing our competition rover for the European Rover Challenge 2025. We've successfully presented our plan to the students of our study programme and are ready to welcome new members! Our rover is designed for fully autonomous operation. We use our expertise in autonomous robotic systems designed for use on Earth and look for ways to use them effectively on other celestial bodies.

More at: [brnomarsrover.cz](https://brnomarsrover.cz)

## TU Brno Racing

![TU Brno Racing logo](/images/logo-tu-brno-racing.webp){width="200"}

Formula Student (FS) is the European offshoot of the originally American Formula SAE competition. It's a prestigious competition for university teams made up of bachelor's and master's students.

Our team, TU Brno Racing, is made up of students from the faculties of Brno University of Technology. We get expert support and advice from doctoral students as well as from department heads and external experts, not only from the automotive industry.

Our main goal is to design, build and test a single-seat racing car to take part in international competitions held in various corners of the world. However, it's not just this motivation that drives our racing team forward: the desire for knowledge, gaining experience in the field and working in a promising team are also our personal engines for reaching this goal: to build the car and be a good team!

More at: [tubrnoracing.cz](https://tubrnoracing.cz)

## SPICE

![SPICE logo – Semiconductors, Physics, IC, Electronics](/images/logo-spice.webp){width="260"}

At a time when semiconductors power everything from smartphones to space missions and determine the technological maturity of nations, a huge opportunity is opening up for a new generation of experts. SPICE, one of the youngest student teams at BUT, brings together enthusiastic students from several faculties who are interested in chips and semiconductors.

Team members don't just gain theoretical knowledge, but above all practical skills through joint projects and close cooperation with academia and industry. SPICE thus creates an inspiring environment for professional growth and even for incubating startup ideas.

You can get involved in a wide range of activities with us. Our internal activities cover everything from building a Student FAB and creating our own knowledge base to designing digital chips. If you're more into communication and popularising the field, you'll find your place in our external activities. You can help make a student semiconductor podcast, prepare educational materials or represent the team at public events.

As a member you can also travel to prestigious events abroad, so join us and be part of it!
