Times move on, and so do we. Away with outdated and confusing platforms! Discord, which probably every gamer knows, lets us help each other, and not just with studying, at any hour.

Want to go to the pub and have no one to go with? Want to find new friends? Talk to someone with similar interests? You can do all of this in our great community. We welcome guests from other faculties and universities too, so feel free to invite friends who don't study at FEEC or are only thinking about it.

Discord is a modern and friendly environment that lets us create lots of places where you can sort out your problems, neatly divided by topic. So you can help each other with maths or physics with your classmates, or just chat, and two things never get discussed in the same place at once.

## Where and how do I get Discord?

There's a Discord app for PC, Mac, Linux, iOS and Android, but you can also log in through a web browser, for example on a computer at the university.

## How do I get started with Discord?

Registering is easy and there's nothing to worry about.
You don't have to use your name or university ID at all. Your nickname can be anything you like.

Register at [discord.com/register](https://discord.com/register). Fill in your email, username and password (no need to register with your university email). Tick that you agree to the terms. Once you've registered, just verify your registration email by clicking the link in the email Discord sends you.

## How do I get on the FEEC Discord?

At [sps-fekt.cz/discord](https://sps-fekt.cz/discord) you'll find, besides a guide, an invitation that takes you to us. Then all you need to do is verify yourself.

## Verification

Because proactive teachers or guests who don't study here can also find their way to us, you need to verify yourself before we unlock the server in all its glory. That way nobody sees what they shouldn't.

All the administrators are students like you, so there's nothing to worry about.

## How do I verify myself?

In the #jail channel, type the command:

```text
-verify vutID@vut.cz
```

E.g.: `-verify 974565@vut.cz`

The verification will arrive in Outlook or Gmail, depending on what you've set up in the information system.

Copy the code from the email and type this command in the #jail channel:

```text
-submit code
```

E.g.: `-submit 1234ABCD`

If you can't get verified, message someone with the MOD or SUBMOD role (at the very top of the user list in the right panel).
